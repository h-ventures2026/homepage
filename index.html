F01

Hunmo Kang

Founder · CEO
LinkedIn

The person who was first to carve a path between technology and capital in Korean venture history.

Widely known as Korea's first patent-attorney venture capitalist. Hunmo Kang earned his patent attorney license while still an undergraduate at Seoul National University's Electrical Engineering department — a rare combination of an engineer's grasp of technology and a lawyer's instinct for structuring rights.

In 2013 he joined ID Ventures as the first patent attorney to serve as an investment principal in Korean VC history, pioneering IP-based investing in Korea. In 2018 he co-founded Hana Ventures as a founding member and registered director, serving as Chief Investment Officer and leading flagship funds including the Hana Digital Transformation Fund (Korea Growth Investment Corp.), Hana Innovation Venture Scale-up Fund (Korea Fund of Funds), Hana K-New Deal Unicorn Fund (Korea Development Bank), and Hana Transcending Frontier Fund of Funds.

From technology- and IP-driven investing to consumer goods and cosmetics, from early-stage rounds to listed-company mezzanine — he has been the rare all-round investor who covers every chapter of a company's life. The companies he has walked with span a generation of Korean technology: KOSPI listings such as Krafton and Shift Up; the KOSDAQ track of Nara Space Technology, Curocell, Cellbion, EnCell, New Kids On, OSP, and Bonne; landmark global strategic exits including Royal Crow (acquired by Tencent) and Red Sahara Studio (acquired by Krafton); and high-conviction names still in motion — Lablup, The-AIO, Exocobio, Brightonix Imaging, Zigbang, Wadiz, FastFive, Laundrygo, Flow, TeamBlind, and Sirloin. In biohealthcare, Nexa I — a novel anti-cancer therapeutics company — stands as a representative long-term companionship.

He also served as Adjunct Professor at Korea University's Graduate School of Technology Management. In February 2026, he founded H Ventures to build a firm run end-to-end on a single coherent philosophy: patient capital, walking together.

03Philosophy

Technology is
rewriting
the world.

AI is rewriting the grammar of every industry. In this transition, capital alone is no longer enough — the best investors must first be fluent in the technology itself.

H Ventures was founded by four partners: Korea's first patent-attorney venture capitalist, an MIT Ph.D. who helped launch the world's first 3nm Gate-All-Around logic, and two of the country's most recognized intellectual-property strategists in AI and biotech. We are not passive capital providers — we debate technology in the founder's own language, design patent portfolios alongside them, and help shape the company that will emerge from it.

Our commitment is simple: we look at people before numbers, ask about fundamentals before trends, and build companies that will still matter in a decade. For founders with real technical moats, we aim to be the first call — and the last one standing.

05Strategies

More than capital.
A strategy.

I.

Technology Insight

Deep tech, not as a slogan. On-the-ground at NVIDIA GTC, reading every US big-tech earnings call, scanning the global research that maps where the frontier is moving — we find the companies one step ahead of the market. Patent-data analytics turn the future into a statistical problem.

II.

IP-Driven Investing

Great founders think about patents before they incorporate, and file them right after. We catch them at that decisive moment — through the eyes of patent attorneys Jongwoo Ryu and Daeho Lee. Post-investment, an IP package is custom-architected to build a moat no one can copy.

III.

EIR & Company Building

Sometimes the right answer is not to find a company — it's to build one. Through our Entrepreneur-in-Residence program, we partner with proven operators to design companies from day zero. Hypothesis testing, founding-team assembly, seed capital, IP architecture — every first button, owned and operated by people who have done it before.

IV.

Concentrated Conviction

We honor the Buffett–Munger philosophy. Identify the rare company with an exceptional team and an unimitable moat — then back it relentlessly, from Series A all the way to unicorn and decacorn. Not diversification, but conviction. Not a quick exit, but a long walk.

V.

The Most AI-Native VC

Sourcing, diligence, documentation, portfolio monitoring — AI is embedded in every workflow. The first Korean VC built end-to-end on AI-native operations. Same hours, more companies seen. Same headcount, deeper diligence.

No. 01

Brightonix Imaging

Korea's only manufacturer of clinical PET systems — seeing dementia and cancer earlier.

A spin-off from Seoul National University Hospital's Functional & Molecular Systems Imaging Lab. Built on roughly 25 years of research (260+ papers, 40+ patents), Brightonix is the only Korean company developing, manufacturing, and selling clinical and preclinical molecular imaging equipment (PET).

Its flagship product, PHAROS, is the first domestically developed clinical PET system to receive medical-device manufacturing certification — and in August 2025, also received U.S. FDA 510(k) clearance. The same week, Brightonix became the first Korean medical device manufacturer to receive Digital Medical Device GMP certification. A next-generation small-animal PET/CT was unveiled to the global market the same year.

A company tackling one of the great medical challenges of an aging society — dementia — through a new standard of brain-dedicated PET. The company we have chosen to write our first page with.

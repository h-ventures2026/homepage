01About

Built by people
who speak technology.

H Ventures is a Seoul-based venture capital firm founded by professionals who have built their careers at the forefront of technology. We bring deep expertise across semiconductors, artificial intelligence, biohealthcare, and intellectual property.

Founded in February 2026, H Ventures invests across all stages—from early-stage startups to pre-IPO companies—and partners with founders throughout their journey. Our ambition goes beyond writing the first check. We strive to be the partner founders call first when facing their most important opportunities and challenges.

02Team

A new firm,
built on long journeys.

H Ventures is a venture capital firm founded by partners who have spent their careers building expertise at the intersection of capital markets and technology. Our team brings together complementary perspectives: the investment judgment of venture capitalists who have managed the full lifecycle of technology investments—from sourcing and due diligence to exit—the hands-on experience of engineers who have successfully commercialized cutting-edge innovations in the global semiconductor industry, and the practical expertise of patent professionals who have shaped intellectual property strategies and supported companies through their journey to IPO across the biotechnology and AI sectors. Rather than chasing market trends, we begin by rigorously validating the durability and scalability of technology through deep analysis and disciplined due diligence. Rather than relying on grand narratives, we work alongside founders to design clear paths to value creation and successful exits from the earliest stages. Our goal is to be a trusted investment partner that contributes meaningfully to the long-term growth of exceptional companies.

03Philosophy

Backing the Future
Category Leaders

Sector No.1. We back the teams that will lead their industries—and stay with them for the entire journey.

Our investment philosophy is simple: we focus on companies with the potential to become the leader in their category.

Regardless of industry, we believe the most effective form of investing is to concentrate our conviction behind the teams that have the ability to define and dominate their markets. Exceptional outcomes are rarely created by consensus—they are created by extraordinary founders and teams with the ambition to lead.

Even when a company is still outside the spotlight, our ultimate investment criterion remains the same: people. Breakthrough technologies and innovative business models alone are not enough. Long-term success depends on the determination, resilience, and execution capability of the people building the company.

We do not chase short-term trends. Instead, we seek companies with durable competitive advantages and management teams capable of reshaping industries and winning markets. We partner with founders who possess both vision and the ability to execute, supporting them through every stage of their journey.

By standing alongside tomorrow’s category leaders from the beginning, we aim to help build companies whose success becomes impossible for the market to ignore.

05Strategies

More than capital.
Strategies.

I.

Technology Insight

Deep tech, not as a slogan. On-the-ground at NVIDIA GTC, reading every US big-tech earnings call, scanning the global research that maps where the frontier is moving — we find the companies one step ahead of the market. Patent-data analytics turn the future into a statistical problem.

II.

IP-Driven Investing

Great founders think about patents before they incorporate, and file them right after. We catch them at that decisive moment — through the eyes of patent attorneys Jongwoo Ryu and Daeho Lee. Post-investment, an IP package is custom-architected to build a moat no one can copy.

III.

EIR & Company Building

Sometimes the right answer is not to find a company — it's to build one. Through our Entrepreneur-in-Residence program, we partner with proven operators to design companies from day zero. Hypothesis testing, founding-team assembly, seed capital, IP architecture — every first button, owned and operated by people who have done it before.

IV.

Concentrated Conviction

We honor the Buffett–Munger philosophy. Identify the rare company with an exceptional team and an unimitable moat — then back it relentlessly, from Series A all the way to unicorn and decacorn. Not diversification, but conviction. Not a quick exit, but a long walk.

V.

The Most AI-Native VC

Sourcing, diligence, documentation, portfolio monitoring — AI is embedded in every workflow. The first Korean VC built end-to-end on AI-native operations. Same hours, more companies seen. Same headcount, deeper diligence.

No. 01

Brightonix Imaging

Korea's Only Manufacturer of Clinical PET Systems — Enabling Faster and More Accurate Detection of Cancer and Neurodegenerative Diseases.

Developer of Digital PET (Positron Emission Tomography) Imaging Systems and AI-Powered Medical Software

Brightonix Imaging is a medical technology company founded by a team of experts in nuclear medicine and medical AI, led by CEO Dr. Jae Sung Lee, Professor of Nuclear Medicine at Seoul National University College of Medicine.

Its flagship product, BTXBrain, an AI-powered brain imaging analysis platform, has received regulatory approval from Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS) and secured commercial agreements with leading global healthcare companies, including Siemens Healthineers, GE HealthCare, and United Imaging. These partnerships demonstrate the platform's clinical relevance and commercial viability in the global market. In addition, the company's high-resolution dedicated brain PET system, PHAROS, has obtained both MFDS approval in Korea and FDA 510(k) clearance in the United States, significantly strengthening its pathway to global commercialization.

The market for neuroimaging diagnostics is entering a period of accelerated growth, driven by the commercialization of novel Alzheimer's disease therapies by global pharmaceutical companies. H Ventures invested in Brightonix Imaging based on the strength of its proprietary technology, the high barriers to replication surrounding its platform, and the management team's proven ability to execute. We are committed to supporting the company as a long-term partner throughout its global scale-up journey.
